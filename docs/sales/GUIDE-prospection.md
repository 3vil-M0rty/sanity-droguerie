# Selling Sanity: prospecting kit (Morocco)

Files in this folder:
- `email-prospection.html`: the designed first email (HTML with inline CSS; works in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail).
- This guide: subject lines, the plain-text version, follow-ups, WhatsApp and LinkedIn messages, a short call script, **where to find prospects**, legal rules and sending set-up.

Attach `../Sanity-Grille-tarifaire.pdf`, or better, the one downloaded from the platform with your details.

---

## 1. Why there is no ready-made list of e-mail addresses

- **Bought or scraped lists don't work.** Many addresses are dead, and many are spam traps. After a few hundred bounces, Gmail and Outlook send **all** your mail to spam, including your invoices to paying clients.
- **The law.** Under law **09-08**, an e-mail address that identifies a person (`karim.benali@…`) is personal data. Prospecting by e-mail is only acceptable to **professional** addresses, about their **professional activity**, with your **identity**, a **way to opt out**, and a **source** you can explain. A list bought from an unknown source fails that test. Have the lawyer confirm the exact rule when they review `docs/legal/`.
- **It sells better.** For a product at 2,990 MAD a month, 50 well-chosen wholesalers contacted one by one beat 5,000 addresses blasted at once.

Build your own list (section 6). It takes about 2 hours for the first 100 good prospects.

## 2. Subject lines (test 2 or 3)

1. `{{Societe}} : stock, commerciaux et encaissements dans un seul outil`
2. `Une question sur vos dépôts, {{Prenom}}`
3. `Vos chèques et LCN sont-ils encore suivis sur Excel ?`
4. `Logiciel marocain pour grossistes : 30 jours d'essai sur vos données`
5. `Ruptures de stock chez {{Societe}} ?`

Keep subjects short, with no capitals and no "!!!". Avoid "gratuit" and "promo" in the subject line: spam filters penalise them.

## 4. Follow-ups (short, plain text, in the same thread)

**Follow-up 1 — day 3**
```
Bonjour {{Prenom}},

Je me permets de remonter mon message. Une seule question : comment suivez-vous aujourd'hui le stock de vos différents dépôts et les commandes de vos commerciaux ?

Si c'est un sujet chez {{Societe}}, je vous montre en 30 minutes comment d'autres distributeurs le font avec Sanity.

[Prénom]
```

**Follow-up 2 — day 7 (a concrete example)**
```
Bonjour {{Prenom}},

Un exemple concret : un grossiste en sanitaire de Casablanca avec 3 dépôts et 6 commerciaux.
Avant : commandes reçues sur WhatsApp, ressaisies le soir, ruptures découvertes au moment de livrer.
Avec Sanity : le commercial prend la commande sur son téléphone chez le client, le dépôt la voit tout de suite, et l'achat est proposé avant la rupture.

Voulez-vous que je vous prépare la même démonstration avec quelques-uns de vos articles ?

[Prénom]
```
*(Replace the example with a real client as soon as you have one. Until then, present it as a typical case, not a reference.)*

**Follow-up 3 — day 14 (last one)**
```
Bonjour {{Prenom}},

Je ne veux pas vous déranger davantage : c'est mon dernier message.
Si la gestion du stock, des commerciaux ou des encaissements devient une priorité chez {{Societe}}, répondez simplement « oui » et je vous rappelle quand cela vous arrange.

Bonne continuation,
[Prénom]
```

No reply after follow-up 3: stop. Try again in 6 months with something new (for example, the e-invoicing date that applies to them).

## 6. Where to find prospects (build your own list)

**Who buys Sanity first.** Wholesalers and distributors with stock, several warehouses or a counter, sales reps and deliveries:
- sanitary ware, plumbing, hardware, electrical equipment, paint;
- building materials, tiles;
- auto parts;
- food distribution, drinks;
- cleaning and hygiene products;
- cosmetics and pharmacy-parapharmacy distribution;
- office supplies, packaging.

Their size: 10 to 200 employees.

**Where they are:**
- **Casablanca**: Derb Omar, Aïn Sebaâ, Lissasfa, Sidi Maârouf, Bouskoura;
- **Tangier**, **Marrakech**, **Fès**, **Agadir**, **Rabat-Salé-Témara**, **Meknès**, **Oujda**, **Kénitra**.

**Legitimate sources of company contacts:**

| Source | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Brand websites** (Grohe, Legrand, Schneider, Colorado, Roca…) | The "official distributors" or "where to buy" pages list wholesalers by city, with their contact details. This is the best source: these companies are exactly your target. |
| **Google Maps** | Search "grossiste sanitaire Casablanca", "distributeur matériel électrique Tanger"… and note the website, phone and general e-mail. |
| **Business directories**: Kerix, Kompass Maroc, Telecontact (Moroccan yellow pages), Charika.ma | Activity, city, size, the manager's name, general contact details. |
| **LinkedIn** (Sales Navigator, about 80 USD a month, has a free trial) | Find the **manager** or **financial director** of the companies on your list. Connect first, write afterwards. |
| **Trade fairs**: Salon International du Bâtiment (Casablanca), sector fairs, CCIS events (the regional chambers of commerce) | Visit the stands with the price list printed. A face-to-face meeting converts ten times better than an e-mail. |
| **Your network and your first clients** | Ask every satisfied client: "Which other wholesaler do you know who would need this?" Offer a month free for each referral that signs. |

## 7. Rules (law 09-08 and good practice)

- **Professional addresses only**, and a message about their professional activity.
- **Always show who you are**: your company's legal name, address and ICE in the footer (already in the template).
- **Easy opt-out** ("répondez STOP"). Honour it **immediately**, and keep the address in a "do not contact" list so you never write to it again.
- **Keep the source** of every contact (the tracker column).
- **Don't buy lists of personal e-mails.** Don't use addresses taken from someone's personal social media.
- Declare your prospecting to the CNDP (it is in part A, processing A1, of `docs/legal/01-CNDP-dossier.md`).

## 8. Sending without landing in spam

1. **Your own domain** (`contact@sanity.ma` or similar), never a Gmail or Hotmail address. Google Workspace or Zoho Mail costs around 30–70 MAD per user per month.
2. Have your web host set up **SPF, DKIM and DMARC** on the domain (15 minutes; Google and Zoho walk you through it).
3. **Start slowly**: 20–30 personalised e-mails a day for the first 2 weeks, then up to 50–80. Never send hundreds at once from your mailbox.
4. **Personalise the first sentence** (city, brand they distribute, something seen on their website). That is what gets replies.
5. **Plain text for first contacts** (section 3) gets more replies. Use the **HTML version** (`email-prospection.html`) after a call, at a trade fair, or for newsletters to contacts who know you.
6. For volume, use **Brevo** (French, free up to 300 e-mails a day) or Mailchimp, import only your own list, and paste the HTML in "code your own".
7. **Measure**: replies per 100 e-mails, demos booked, trials started. A good B2B campaign gets 5–10 % replies and 2–4 % demos.

## 9. How to use the HTML e-mail

- **Gmail (manual):** open `email-prospection.html` in Chrome, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste into a new Gmail message. The layout survives. Replace `{{Prenom}}`, `{{Societe}}` and `{{Ville}}` by hand.
- **Brevo or Mailchimp:** choose "HTML template" or "code your own", paste the file's content, then replace the tags with the tool's own: Brevo `{{ contact.PRENOM }}`, Mailchimp `*|FNAME|*`.
- **Before the first send**, replace everything in `[brackets]`:
  - your name, phone and e-mail;
  - the **appointment link** (a free Calendly or Google Calendar "appointment schedule" works);
  - your legal name, address and ICE in the footer.
- **Send yourself a test** on Gmail, Outlook and a phone before sending to anyone.
